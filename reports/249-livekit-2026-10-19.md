## C) Level and Strategy

**JD level:** unstated. The $135K floor implies LiveKit hires below senior, and the "exceptional" language and framework-design scope point to senior in practice.
**Natural level for this archetype:** senior on state/concurrency and distributed messaging; mid or below on LLM/agent framework work. A realistic landing is **mid-level on the Agents team**, and that is still at or above his $80K–$120K target.

**Sell senior without lying:**
- Lead with **state and concurrency under live traffic**: "I kept two production databases in two-way sync over Pub/Sub while 20+ apps moved across with zero downtime. Durable agent sessions are the same problem: ordering, partial failure, resumption."
- Second, **text channels**: the WhatsApp assistant (LLM + vision + conversation state inside a messaging platform's limits) and the USSD service (text sessions over unreliable carrier networks). The JD names SMS explicitly. This is his best hook.
- Third, **agent judgment**: the evidence-gated pipeline and the human-on-Submit rule show he has thought about where agents should and should not act. That fits "exploring emerging patterns rather than simply applying established ones".
- Fourth, **docs for other engineers**: the Firebase SDK documentation and Ops training.

**Forbidden framings:** "built production agents", "built an agent framework/SDK", "built career-ops" (it is a customisation of santifer's open-source project), "Python expert", "TypeScript (5+ years)", PostgreSQL/SQL engine claims, BigQuery, WebRTC (not evidenced; ffmpeg video overlay is not WebRTC).

**If downleveled:** accept if comp is ≥ $100K (the range floor after market adjustment may still be above target). Ask for a 6-month review with ownership of a framework surface (e.g. the text modality) as the promotion criterion.
