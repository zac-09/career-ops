### Level Detection

| **Years** | 5+ years pre-sales | 4+ years engineering | Different experience type |
| **Scope** | C-level relationships, enterprise deals | Team lead (4 devs), CTO reporting | Not equivalent |

### Assessment

This is a **career track change**, not a level match. Solutions Engineering is a distinct career path from Software Engineering:

- **SE (Software Engineer)**: builds products
- **Solutions Engineer**: sells products technically, bridges sales and engineering

Isaac would need to:
1. Reposition as a "technical builder who wants to move customer-facing"
2. Accept entry/mid-level Solutions Engineer role, not Senior
3. Demonstrate customer communication skills (limited evidence in CV)

### Downlevel Strategy (if applicable)

Not recommended to apply. The gaps are structural (location, career track, experience type), not level-related.
