## Headline caveats (read these before the score)

1. **This is a genuine seniority stretch, and the report does not smooth it over.** The JD asks for "7+ years building software, including **2–4+ years leading teams (EM/Tech Lead/Head of Eng)**". Isaac's only people-leadership line on cv.md is CodeBits: "Led a team of 4 developers building legal tech systems for NGOs in Uganda" — January 2020 to July 2021, i.e. **~1.5 years**, against a floor of 2–4+. And the *shape* of the gap is worse than the duration: the req's first responsibility bullet is **hiring, coaching, performance management, career development**. There is not a single line on cv.md evidencing hiring, running a performance cycle, or owning someone's career ladder. Seniority is scored **2.0/5** and that is the honest number. The 120–200 USD/Hr band must not be allowed to inflate it.
2. **Pay is explicitly location-indexed.** Body, verbatim: "**120 – 200USD/Hr, based on experience and location**". For a Kampala-based candidate that is a geographic-discount clause in the posting itself, and Glassdoor reviewers report that at G2i "rates appear strictly non-negotiable" — so the pushback has to happen at the *screen*, before a band is assigned, not at offer. Script in §D.
3. **Contract vs full-time contradiction, scored on the worse reading.** The Ashby chip says Employment Type "Full time"; the body says "**Contract leadership roles considered (up to 6 months)**". Stability is scored on the **≤6-month** reading. A six-month contract to hire, coach, and performance-manage a team is internally incoherent — you cannot run a career-development cycle inside two quarters. This must be resolved at screening question one.
4. **On-call ownership is in scope.** "Own delivery health: sprint/iteration hygiene, predictable execution, and **strong on-call/incident practices**." The JD states no timezone requirement, but owning incident response for a US-client-facing team from UTC+3 has real implications for the working day that the posting does not price in. Ask what the rotation actually looks like.
5. **G2i is a talent marketplace, not a product company.** "G2i helps startups hire senior engineers faster and smarter through video-based technical evaluation. We've placed thousands of developers worldwide since 2016." This req sits in the "G2i Eng Team" department — i.e. building G2i's *own* internal platforms/dashboards — but the employer is an agency/staffing business. That caps both the durability of the seat and the prestige of the line on the CV relative to a direct product employer.

## C) Level and Strategy — the Senior-vs-Staff verdict

**JD level:** Staff / EM. Hard bar 7+ yrs building, 2–4+ yrs leading teams, with hiring and performance management in scope.
**Candidate's natural level for this archetype:** **Senior full-stack / backend IC, with credible tech-lead range.** ~6.7 years building (effectively clears the 7). ~1.5 years of people leadership, five years stale (does not clear 2–4+). Zero hiring or performance-management evidence.

### The verdict: target **#221 (Senior Engineer, 50–150 USD/Hr)**, not this req.

Five reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The bar that is missed is the one that is screened first.** The published process is "Resume submission → **Leadership/technical screen** → Behavioral interview → Offer". The leadership screen is gate one. A candidate with 1.5 stale years of team leadership and no hiring or performance-cycle experience is being filtered on his weakest axis before his strongest axis (shipping production full-stack systems) is ever examined.
2. **The rate bands overlap, so the stretch buys much less than it looks like.** #221 pays **50–150 USD/Hr**; this req pays **120–200 USD/Hr**. The Senior ceiling ($150) sits *above* the Staff floor ($120). A strong Senior placement at or near the top of #221's band lands within the Staff band's lower half — without requiring Isaac to defend a leadership record he does not have. The marginal gain from the stretch is the difference between $150 and whatever a location-indexed band would actually offer him, which given "based on experience and location" is plausibly *below* $150 anyway.
3. **The job itself is not the job he wants right now.** Responsibilities 1 and 7 are people management; this is an EM req with a Staff title. profile.yml targets "Senior Backend Engineer / Senior Software Engineer" as primary, with "Engineering Manager" nowhere in his archetype list. Winning this seat would mean spending six months on hiring and performance cycles rather than on the building work his CV is compounding.
4. **Six months is too short for the leadership mandate.** "Contract leadership roles considered (up to 6 months)" against "hiring, coaching, performance management, career development" is an incoherent pairing. Whoever takes this on a contract will be measured on delivery health, not on the people outcomes the req advertises — meaning the CV line earned may not match the CV line promised.
5. **Same company, same board, one application.** Applying to both reqs at G2i signals either indiscriminate application or an unclear self-assessment. Pick one. **Pick #221.**

**If Isaac applies here anyway** (defensible only as a deliberate "aim high on the same board" move, and only if #221 is submitted first or not at all):

- **Sell senior without lying — permitted framings, all CV-backed:**
  - "I led the end-to-end migration of 20+ applications from Parse/MongoDB to Firebase/GCP with zero downtime, reporting directly to the CTO." — programme ownership at Staff scope.
  - "I led a team of 4 developers building legal tech systems for NGOs, and architected the microservices backend on Kafka, Docker and Kubernetes." — team leadership *plus* architecture, which is the Staff shape.
  - "I've worked full-stack React and Node.js in four of my five roles, and I've shipped revenue features — Express Shipping added $40 per order — and cut $5,000/month of infrastructure cost."
  - "Three of my five roles reported directly to a CTO with no layer in between."
- **Forbidden framings:** any phrasing that implies 2+ years of current people management; any implication of hiring or performance-management ownership; any claim of React 19/SSR or tRPC production experience; upgrading TypeScript beyond what cv.md supports.
- **The downlevel plan is the actual plan.** If G2i says "you're not there for Staff", the correct response is immediate and prepared: *"Agreed — I'd like to be considered for the Senior Engineer req on the same board instead. My strength is shipping production full-stack systems, and that's the seat where I'd be contributing from week one."* This converts a rejection into a referral inside the same company. **Rehearse this answer before the screen.**
